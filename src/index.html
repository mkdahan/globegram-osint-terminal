How to connect (one-time setup):
  1. Open my.telegram.org and log in with your phone number.
  2. Click API development tools, create an app (any name), and copy the api_id and api_hash.
  3. Paste them below and save. They are stored only on this computer (%LOCALAPPDATA%\globegram-terminal) — never inside the project folder or on GitHub.
  4. Enter your phone number with country code (e.g. +972501234567) and press Send code.
  5. Telegram sends a login code to your Telegram app (not SMS) — type it here and sign in.
  6. If your account has two-step verification, you will also be asked for your 2FA password.
Your session is saved, so next launches connect automatically. This is your own personal account — the app only reads channels you already joined.
